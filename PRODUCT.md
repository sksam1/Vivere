# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective coaching clients evaluating Samuel Korgi's VIVERE wellness and
human-performance practice. Two segments per `content.ts` tracks: Track A
("Wellness Core", 80%) — individuals wanting foundational health/habit
coaching rooted in discipline and faith; Track B ("Performance Extension",
20%) — founders/executives and organizations wanting the same discipline
applied to leadership and teams. They typically arrive via social/referral,
read the positioning and the coach's story, and decide whether to submit an
inquiry. The job to be done: judge in a few minutes of scrolling whether this
coach's philosophy and credibility match what they're looking for, then act
(inquiry form) or leave.

## Product Purpose

A single-page marketing site that converts visitors into inquiries via the
contact form (`app/api/inquiry/route.ts`). Success = a visitor who reads the
ethos, the four-phase method, the coins/creed, and the coach's bio, then
submits a genuine inquiry (or at minimum leaves trusting the brand). Not an
e-commerce or booking flow; it is a persuasion and trust-building surface
that funnels to one conversion point.

## Brand Personality

Roman / Stoic / timeless. Gold-inscriptional type on obsidian black, built
around Samuel's three physical challenge coins ("carried, not framed").
Positioning: health as the core, discipline as the edge. Faith (Christian) is
present as a real anchor, stated plainly, not preached. Voice is disciplined,
declarative, unhurried, confident without hype. Confirmed as a brand site
whose primary goal is inquiry generation.

## Anti-references

No named anti-references supplied; default to rejecting the general AI-slop
and category-reflex patterns (see the Impeccable skill's absolute bans):
generic fitness-influencer neon/hustle energy, stock protein-shake and
gym-bro photography, countdown-timer urgency, SaaS-style pricing cards,
gradient text, tiny tracked eyebrows on every section, side-stripe cards.
This is a premium, restrained, editorial brand site, not a lead-gen funnel
template.

## Design Principles

- Health as the core, discipline as the edge — wellness leads, discipline
  supports; don't let the site tip into pure "grind culture" fitness-bro
  tone.
- Carried, not framed — the coins are pocket discipline, not merch; language
  and presentation should stay tactile and personal, not transactional.
- Self-reliance over dependence — the four-phase method promises graduation,
  not a lifetime retainer; the site's tone should reflect confidence in the
  client's own capability, not upsell pressure.
- Faith as anchor, not preachy — present plainly (Philippians 4:13 coin,
  "faith rhythm" in discovery) without evangelizing.
- Timeless over trendy — Roman inscriptional aesthetic (Cinzel + EB Garamond,
  gold on obsidian) should read as permanent, not as a 2026 web-design trend.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA bar: 4.5:1 text contrast (3:1 for large text), full
keyboard operability, visible focus states, meaningful alt text on imagery.
`prefers-reduced-motion` is already implemented site-wide (Ember field,
gold-sheen animation, Lenis smooth scroll, global animation-duration
override) and should be preserved as the accessibility floor. No further
accessibility requirements specified by the owner.
